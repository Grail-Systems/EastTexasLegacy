State water science

  • TWDB Report 110: Ground-Water Conditions in Angelina and Nacogdoches Counties

    The state’s 1970 study of our two counties. Pumping had pulled the Carrizo down nearly 500 feet, and the state warned there was room for only about 5 million more gallons a day. Our drawdown model uses its numbers.

    Texas Water Development Board, March 1970 · PDF
  • TWDB Report 327: Ground water around Lufkin, Nacogdoches, Tyler and nearby cities

    Named the area between Lufkin and Nacogdoches one of East Texas’s two worst drawdown areas, pointing to the paper mills.

    Texas Water Development Board, Feb. 1991 · PDF
  • The Status of Brackish Aquifer Studies in Texas

    Salty groundwater under us hasn’t been studied as a water supply, so it isn’t a ready option. See the state’s zone designations.

    Texas Water Development Board, Dec. 1, 2025 · PDF
  • East Texas Regional Water Plan, Chapter 5B: Angelina County

    Plans for new industry to buy water from the City, with Sam Rayburn as the long-term supply.
